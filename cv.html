Areas of research / interests

She investigates the intersection between contemporary art and philosophy, and how this relationship transforms our perception of ourselves. Her work combines a background in philosophy and aesthetics with an approach informed by neuroscience and cognition, paying particular attention to how perception is constructed and altered through image, technology, and sensory stimulus.

Conceptual axis of her practice

Her practice sits at the intersection of philosophy of art, research in cognition and perception, and photographic and art direction work. She is particularly interested in how image and sensory stimulus construct and transform perception, exploring narratives that touch on intimacy, memory, technology, and spatial experience.

Academic background

  • Master's in Brain and Cognition — UPF, 2025. Master's thesis developed at the Multisensory Research Group (MRG) at UPF, focused on how stimuli of differing congruence affect the brain.
  • Degree in Philosophy, specializing in Contemporary Art and Aesthetics — UNED, 2023. Undergraduate thesis: research on contemporary art and technology, and how this intersection transforms perception and identity.
  • Degree in Photography — IEFC, 2023.

Professional positions

She currently works as a Product Manager at Filmin. She also co-directs a curatorial and workshop space in Guinardó (Barcelona) together with two friends.

Featured work

Styling and creative direction for La Niña, a dance piece created by Aurora Vallejo as her final project at the Institut del Teatre. To shape the piece, Aurora and Karmele researched its visual language together through a moodboard drawing on references such as Persona (Ingmar Bergman), Kate Bush, Issey Miyake, and The Strange Case of Angelica, which established the visual foundation of the presentation.

The piece explores a complex mother-daughter relationship, marked by moments of closeness and distance. Through projections, Aurora's face is overlaid with her mother's, using technology as a tool to materialize this shifting bond between them.
