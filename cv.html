karmele ustarroz

barcelona

Creative researcher and visual practitioner working across art direction, photography, and contemporary visual culture. Product Manager at Filmin and co-coordinator of a curatorial and workshop space in Horta-Guinardó. Her work investigates the intersection between contemporary art and philosophy, exploring how image, technology, and sensory stimulus construct and transform perception.

k.ustarroz@gmail.com / @karmele__
